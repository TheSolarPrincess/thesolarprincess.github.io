Sunspots of 19/11/2016

Links to various random things and corresponding thoughts that don’t deserve their own blogposts.

Russian indigenous Chukchi people change their genders on the orders of spirits. I wonder if trans people living in Chukotka could use that as a legal justification to transition.

China, Japan, and India had pig-based toilets, using excrement-eating pigs for waste management. No data about possible recursive pig toilets for the pigs’ excrement.

It’s commonly believed that, due to patriarchy or something, "female" diseases are underresearched. This is wrong: here is, for example, an endometriosis study, in which the scientists determined that looks closely women with endometriosis are prettier. Hm. To be fair, the study is retracted. But even in the retraction notice, the scientists insist that they did everything right, and they only retract because of "distress to some people". "Women's respect is a priority for us", they say, carefully selecting the exact phrasing that makes it ambiguous if they mean "us respecting women" or "women respecting us". What a bunch of miserable assholes…

Dragonsweeper is a tiny roguelike puzzle game that you can play in the browser. It’s an excellent example of how you can take a terse idea and some very simple base mechanics and make them into a great game. I recommend trying it out.

Selfies as a second language – an interesting observation about the differing psychology of people who saw their own faces mainly in the mirror vs mainly on photos. I can’t personally know if that’s true – with my severe face blindness, I see a stranger in the mirror. But it sounds curious.

A blogger explains why passkeys are a trap and you shouldn’t use them.

A hillarious list of cases when an optimization algorithm does screws up its own target value and does something unexpected. Sort of a micro-version of the hypothetical AI doom scenario. My favorite is when an evolutionary algorithm tasked with creating a fast animal, created a tall animal that can topple very fast indeed.

Did you know how pen caps work? I thought I knew, but I didn’t.

Politics

"The laws of mathematics are very commendable, but the only law that applies in Australia is the law of Australia" said an Australian prime minister. Could technically be true if the law of Australia contains the laws of mathematics and physics. I’m not a lawyer so I can’t confirm or deny.

American police uses the latest achievements of surveillance capitalism to ruin the lives of random personal enemies. Who could have thought that giving cops more power means they will hurt more innocents?! If anyone still subscribes to the "nothing to hide, nothing to fear" argument, you have to understand that ACAB.

On the official website of the White House, there is a page with vibe coded propaganda video games. Yes, really. I doubt that anyone plays them; they were probably made to placate Trump.

Before the UN conference Bill Gates talks about climate change. Translation from billionaire to human: "we can no longer hide that climate change is a real problem, even the slowest and the dumbest can see through the propaganda 😩 but please, never try to solve it with any methods that threaten my power 😭 do something that gives me more money and allows me to control your life more, like tech innovations and private charity ☺️ yes, sure, every time in the past when you gave us more power to solve a problem, that problem only got worse, but this time I pinky promise it will work trust me on this one"

When capitalism fans, blind to the notion of political power, talk about the bourgeoisie, they often reduce all left-wing problems with the Powers That Be to envy about not having a yacht; or explain that just because someone is richer doesn’t mean you’re poorer. But when actual anti-capitalists talk about the bourgeoisie, they explain in great detail specifically how specific billionaires ruined their lives and made the world a worse place. I am scared how easy it is for this kind of thing to work as a thought-terminating cliche. I remember that, soon after the last election of Trump, I expressed concern about Peter Thiel’s collaboration with the government, and my (ancap-identifying!) friend reflexively rounded my words to this exact strawman. I don’t know who invented this propaganda meme, but it works!
